Today I received an email from Paddle — the payment provider behind AnkiMCP subscriptions — saying they've closed our account effective immediately, after a review. No specific reason was given beyond their standard categories; I've asked for details and will update this post if I learn more.

## What this means for you, practically

- **Nothing changes about the service.** Hosted Anki, sync, your decks, your data — all unaffected. This is a payments issue, not a service issue.
- **Existing subscribers:** your current paid period stays active in full. Since Paddle can no longer bill, renewals simply won't be charged until we're live on a new payment provider — you will not lose access, and you won't be double-billed later.
- **New subscriptions** are paused until the new provider is up.

## What I'm doing

I'm evaluating replacements now (Stripe is the leading candidate) and expect payments to be back within a few weeks. If your subscription lapses in the meantime, I'll extend it manually — nobody gets cut off because of this.

Small projects get flagged by payment-risk systems all the time, often with no explanation and little recourse. It's a known hazard of building on a merchant-of-record — I'm writing this partly so the next indie dev who gets this email knows they're not alone.
