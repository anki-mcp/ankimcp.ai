New to AnkiMCP? There are **three ways to use it**. Pick the one that fits you — you can always mix and match later.

## 1. Connect an AI to your local Anki (free)

Keep Anki on your own computer and let an AI assistant create and review cards for you. It's **free and open source**, and you can connect with either the add-on or the command-line tool.

The fastest way to start:

- [Connect Claude to Anki](/docs/how-to/connect-claude/)
- [Connect ChatGPT to Anki](/docs/how-to/connect-chatgpt-to-anki/)
- [Connect Cursor, Cline & other coding tools](/docs/how-to/connect-mcp-clients/)

## 2. Anki Studio (coming soon)

Anki Studio will store your decks and study materials for you and help you plan what to learn next. **It's coming soon** — watch this space.

## 3. Hosted Anki (Pro plan)

Want your Anki available even when your laptop is off? [**Hosted Anki**](/docs/hosted-anki/) runs a real Anki in the cloud on our servers, so your AI assistant can reach it any time — no computer left running at home. It's part of the Pro plan.
