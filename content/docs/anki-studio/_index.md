Anki Studio is AnkiMCP's cloud side: features your AI reaches through a single **Studio MCP endpoint**, separate from the tunnel that connects it to the Anki on your computer. The tunnel reaches your local Anki; Studio reaches your account — today that means your **knowledge roadmap** and your **media library**, with more to come.
